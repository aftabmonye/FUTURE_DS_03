# 📊 Marketing Funnel Performance Dashboard

A modern and interactive **Marketing Funnel Performance Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze the complete customer acquisition journey. This dashboard provides a clear view of marketing performance by tracking visitors, leads, conversions, revenue, marketing spend, and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

---

## 📸 Dashboard Preview

![Marketing Funnel Dashboard](dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to help marketing teams monitor the effectiveness of their campaigns, understand customer conversion patterns, optimize marketing spend, and improve overall business performance through data-driven insights.

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 👥 Total Visitors
- 🎯 Total Leads
- ✅ Total Qualified Leads
- 🛍️ Total Customers
- 💰 Total Revenue
- 📈 Marketing Spend
- 📊 Overall ROAS

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Marketing Funnel
Visualizes the complete customer journey:
- Visitors
- Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Customers

### Revenue vs Marketing Spend
- Monthly revenue trend
- Monthly marketing spend comparison

### ROAS by Channel
Performance comparison across:
- Paid Search
- Social Media
- Email
- Display Ads

### Conversion by Campaign
Analyze campaign-wise conversion performance.

### Revenue by Region
Compare revenue generated across different regions.

### Revenue by Device
Revenue distribution by:
- Mobile
- Desktop
- Tablet

### Marketing Spend by Channel
Understand spending across various marketing channels.

### Average CTR Analysis
Compare Click Through Rate (CTR) performance across campaigns.

### Interactive Filters
- Date Range
- Campaign
- Channel
- Region
- Device
